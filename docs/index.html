Asmodeus ०

A new machine-learning architecture. A softmax decider, the time traveller, answers alone, and goes back to an era of the past when it needs that era's knowledge. Its weight over the eras is a fluid that moves with momentum.

On streams whose laws recur, its regret is 11× below the best learner we built to compete with it, and 15× below a meta-trained transformer that reads the regime from its context. It forgets nothing on re-entry and invents no eras on stationary data. The seven lemmas behind the bank and the decider are checked in Lean. Every bar was frozen by sha256 before its result existed.

Every result on this page is on synthetic streams built to favour eras. They are switching order-1 Markov sources over 16 symbols. They show that the mechanism works where it should. They say nothing yet about language. Real text is stage 7 of the roadmap, and the comparison with small GPTs is stage 8.

1 · The idea, in the notes' own words

The architecture starts from two pages of handwritten notes. Every term on this page is defined from them, and the full transcription is in the repository (origin_notes_transcription.md).

our softmax is a TT (Time Traveller), is smart alone, but when he needs knowledge of a particular era he goes back in time; his TT contains only results of history; the era contains knowledge.

“That if we combine”, page 3

we will provide the time travel a dimensional phase; when this momentum will be like in a dimension of “liquid” … path of softmax … fluid chrononal predictive architecture (after multiplication and normalisation).

“That if we combine”, page 3

Three objects follow, and nothing else is assumed.

  • Era. A stretch of the stream with one fixed law, and the small statistical model that holds that law. Here an era is a 16 × 16 table of transition counts.
  • TT, the time traveller. The softmax decider. It keeps only results of history, a weight per era. It answers with its blend, or alone with one era, and it returns to an old era the moment the stream does.
  • Chronal fluid data density. w_t, the TT's weight over the eras at time t. It is where the TT stands in the “liquid of eras”. Its path is the “path of softmax”, and it carries momentum. (PROGRAM.md, Addendum 2.)

The architecture as it runs today. The bank and the decider are separate: the bank decides which eras exist, and the TT decides how much weight each one gets. Bar heights are illustrative.

2 · The chronal fluid as an update rule

“After multiplication and normalisation” is a Bayes step. Each token, the TT does three things to its weight vector w_t over the pool of experts (the eras, the window model and the novelty expert). This is the code in deciders.py, line for line, and it is the model the Lean proofs are about.

move vₜ = (1 − α)·wₜ + (α/2)·1/n + (α/2)·wₜ Q̃ predict pₜ = Σᵢ vₜ(i) Pₜ(i) update wₜ₊₁(i) = vₜ(i) Pₜ(i) / pₜ
  • Move is where momentum lives. A fraction α of the weight is shared out. Half spreads uniformly. The other half travels along Q̃, a kernel learned from the era switches the bank has seen (switch counts plus one, zero diagonal, rows normalised). Weight flows to the era that usually comes next. That is “where it came from predicts where it goes”.
  • Predict answers with the blend. Pₜ(i) is expert i's probability of the token that arrives.
  • Update multiplies by the likelihood and normalises. Weight concentrates on whichever era explains the recent tokens.

The incumbent it replaced, SOFT, is the same rule with all of α spread uniformly (Herbster–Warmuth fixed share). The winner is called MOM. With Q̃ set to the identity it reduces to SOFT at α/2, and with Q̃ uniform it reduces to SOFT at α; both identities are proved (L41_Momentum.lean). The deployed setting is α = 0.003.

3 · Eras: birth, merge and the novelty expert

The bank runs one bookkeeping loop, shared by every decider. It lives in arms.py (fcpa).

  • Detect. An e-detector (Shin–Ramdas–Rinaldo) watches whether the active era still explains the stream. Ville's inequality bounds a false alarm by δ = 0.05.
  • Wait for evidence. No decision before 64 transitions have accumulated since the change-time estimate.
  • Address. The segment is scored by its prequential likelihood under every stored era and under a fresh one. The best stored era wins a JUMP back to it. A fresh era wins a BIRTH.
  • Merge. After each decision, the pair of eras with the highest Dirichlet–multinomial Bayes factor merges while that factor exceeds 1.
  • Novelty expert. BOCPD sits in the TT's pool, so a law that has never been seen costs little.

It took three preregistered rounds to get the bank right, each on fresh seeds. The table is the record, failures included (VERDICT_E0.md).

4 · Going back in time, measured

This is the TT's defining move, seen on a real run. The stream below switches between four laws, and fifteen of its first nineteen segments return to a law already seen.

Excess loss over an oracle that is told which law is active and keeps one count table per law, in nats per token, smoothed over 100 tokens. REC bed, seed 40 (an evaluation seed), first 6,000 of 20,000 tokens. Computed with the repository's own arms.fcpa and deciders.Momentum at α = 0.003; whole-stream regret 0.027 for the era bank against 0.301 for BOCPD.

  • BOCPD detects each switch, then must relearn the law's 256 transition probabilities from nothing. Its loss spikes at every return to an old law and decays slowly. On a law's first visit it learns as fast as the oracle, so there is no spike.
  • The era bank detects the switch, finds the era that already holds this law, and returns to it. The first 100 tokens after a re-entry cost 0.084 nats over the oracle, against 0.693 for BOCPD and 0.840 for an exponential moving average (RESULTS_E0c.md).
  • Nothing is overwritten. A birth leaves every old era's table exactly as it was. This is a theorem (L40, below), and EMA is its contrast: it keeps λ^L of an era's counts after L steps away.

5 · The decider tournament

Six deciders ran over the same bank, each tuned on its own seeds and evaluated paired on fresh ones. The rule, frozen in advance: a challenger replaces the incumbent only if it passes every gate and beats it on at least 8 of 10 seeds (E0e_PREREG.md).

  • MOM beat SOFT on 10 of 10 seeds, score 0.0486 against 0.0515 (−5.6%). The gain is largest where eras follow a path: CYC −7%, CYC-S −6%, NOREC −8%. It ties or wins on REC, where switches are uniform. Momentum is measurable.
  • CTC, the closed time-like loop, adds nothing as a decider. Lemma L32 predicted it: a contracting loop has a tilt cap w*_max / w*_min ≤ e^{2q} < e², so it cannot route sharply. It keeps its role as the provable return trip, not the answer.
  • HARD answers with one era alone on 45.8% of steps at no measurable loss. That is a compute result: about half the steps need one era.
  • Round 1 is disclosed. Its tuning objective left out NOREC, which pushed every decider to α = 0.01 and broke G2 for five of six. Round 2 changed only the objective and ran on fresh seeds (VERDICT_TOURNAMENT.md).

6 · Against models that read their context

The real competitor is in-context learning. A sequence model can, in principle, infer the current law from what it has just read, with no era machinery at all. E1 tests that directly (VERDICT_E1.md, PR #24).

  • Both bars pass on 10 of 10 seeds. On REC, ERA's regret is 6% of the best neural arm's, 15× lower. On CYC-S it is 22% (worst seed 40%, bar 50%).
  • Why: memory beyond the context window. The transformer sees 512 tokens. On CYC-S that is about five segments, too few to estimate a 16 × 16 table. ERA keeps every regime's counts for the whole stream and returns to them. The two models differ in what they can remember, not in how well they were tuned.
  • The weak arm is stated. GRU-META never learned in context (validation 2.69 nats against the oracle's 1.78). The bars did not depend on it: TF-META and GRU-ONLINE-L were the best arms on every seed.

7 · The theorems, in Lean

The rule of this project: a measured result is not proven. Proven means a lemma and a Lean proof. Seven lemmas cover the bank, the decider and the filter as shipped. They are stated about the code that ran, not an idealisation of it. Lean 4 v4.7.0 with Mathlib; check_no_sorry.py reports 0 sorry (ERA_LEMMAS.md, PR #26, PR #30).

L38 · Regret over a growing bank PROVED

For every token sequence, every expert prediction sequence, every schedule of births and merges, and every comparator path cₜ through the pool:

Σₜ −log pₜ ≤ Σₜ −log Pₜ(cₜ) + log(|s₀| / (1 − α + ρ)) + Σₜ costₜ

Staying on a path costs at most log 1/(1−α) per token, and the same per birth; a switch costs at most log(N/ρ). It holds for any linear decider, SOFT and MOM included. The proof telescopes −log pₜ and shows each transport step keeps weight on the comparator. Checked on 18 runs of the real code: on NOREC the bound is nearly tight, MOM 0.0012–0.0015 measured against 0.0016. L38.bank_regret, window_regret, butterfly · L38_GrowingBankRegret.lean

L39 · The merge rule is the address rule PROVED

Merging era C with era D fires exactly when C beats a fresh era on D's tokens in the address step. The two rules cannot disagree, and the Bayes factor is an e-value: P(BF ≥ 1/δ) ≤ δ under separate laws.

Consistency: log BF = −Div + Gain ± K, where the divergence grows linearly with evidence when laws differ and the Occam gain only logarithmically. So different laws are eventually refused and equal laws merged. L39.merge_iff_address, log_bf_sandwich · L39_MergeRule.lean, L39c_MergeConsistency.lean

Finding. The code's threshold BF > 1 is δ = 1: no finite-sample guarantee. L39d below gives the threshold that has one.

L40 · No forgetting PROVED

No operation the bank performs (credit, birth, merge, re-address) ever loses a settled token. An era no operation touches keeps exactly its table, and therefore its predictive, through an excursion of any length.

The unconditional form is false, and the counterexample is recorded: a re-address can take an unsettled token out of the era it was credited to. The proved form restricts to settled tokens. L40.no_forgetting, untouched_era_unchanged · L40_NoForgetting.lean

L41 · MOM is an HMM forward filter PROVED

A = (1−α)I + (α/2)J/n + (α/2)Q̃ is row-stochastic and v = wA. So the momentum decider is an exact HMM forward filter, and a switch along the learned kernel costs at most −log((α/2)Q̃(j,i)).

Against BOCPD, MOM pays log 1/(1−α/2) per token, half of SOFT. The bound is pathwise with the kernel in force at each step, so the learned Q̃ is covered as it is. L41.mom_eq_hmm_predict, mom_switch_cost · L41_Momentum.lean

L39d · A merge threshold with a guarantee PROVED

Merge at the j-th check only when BF_j ≥ (j+1)(j+2)/δ. Then the probability of any false merge, for any pair, at any time, is at most δ. A single fixed threshold cannot give that guarantee; that is proved too.

L39d.merge_false_alarm, no_uniform_merge_guarantee · L39d_MergeThreshold.lean

L41b · The learned kernel PROVED

For any sequence of era switches, the kernel learned from counts is within (k−1) log(n+1) nats of the best fixed kernel chosen in hindsight.

How fast the learned parameters approach the true switch law is cited, not formalised. L41b.learned_kernel_regret · L41b_LearnedKernel.lean

L42 · The filter as shipped PROVED

The log-space filter with a max-shifted log-sum-exp is exactly L38's Bayes step, with nothing overflowing or underflowing. HARD routing, answering with the top era alone at weight ≥ θ, costs at most log(θ + (1−θ)/ε) per HARD step, so L38 carries over to it.

The floor ε on expert probabilities is load-bearing: the Dirichlet smoothing provides it. L42.logBayes_exact, hard_regret · L42_ShippedFilter.lean

They rest on an earlier chain, L1–L37, in the same folder: among them non-destructive birth (L23), Ville-bounded birth (L24s), exact online fitting (L27) and the HMM filter (L14b).

8 · What the theorems explain

The proofs earn their place by predicting the measurements before anyone tuned to them.

  • Why round 1 broke G2. L38 bounds the overhead against BOCPD by α/(1−α) per token for SOFT and about α/2 for MOM. G2 is therefore guaranteed when α ≤ 0.0039 (SOFT) or α ≤ 0.0078 (MOM). Round 1 picked α = 0.01, outside both, and five of six deciders failed. Round 2's 0.003 is inside both, and both passed.
  • Why momentum pays more on fast cycles. L41 prices each switch along a learned cycle at about 1.0–1.4 nats less than SOFT. The saving should scale with the number of switches, and it does:

10 · Method

  • Bars before results. Every preregistration is frozen by sha256 in FREEZE.txt before the run it judges. An amendment is a new frozen file, never an edit.
  • Fresh seeds only. Tuning seeds and evaluation seeds never overlap. Each round moves to a new block: 0–9, 10–19, 20–29, 30–39, 40–49, 50–59.
  • Owned baselines. Every competitor was built in this repository and scored on the same streams: global order-1 and order-2 models, EMA, BOCPD, an oracle, online and meta-trained GRUs, a meta-trained transformer.
  • Failures stay on the record. E0's two failed bars, E0b's over-splitting, round 1's broken gate and L40's false version are all in the repository.
  • Lemma, Lean, build. The notes put it in four words: “LEMMA and LEAN → BUILD → PROVEN”.

11 · Running it

The era bank and the tournament are numpy only, four CPU cores, about 15 minutes in all.

git clone https://github.com/teerthsharma/Asmodeus
cd Asmodeus/zero/research/e0
python -m pytest -q test_e0.py     # sanity tests
python run.py && python score.py   # E0
python run_e0b.py                  # E0b
python run_e0c.py                  # E0c: the bank of record
python run_e0d.py                  # tournament, round 1
python run_e0d.py e0e              # tournament, round 2: MOM wins

E1 needs torch. The transformer's meta-training took 52 minutes on a CPU.

cd Asmodeus/zero/research/e1
python run_e1.py --smoke           # end-to-end check, numbers mean nothing
python run_e1.py all               # ERA, GRUs, transformer, then the bars

The proofs build with Lake.

cd Asmodeus/zero/lean
lake build
python check_no_sorry.py           # expects 0

13 · Limits

  • Synthetic beds only. Every win is on order-1 Markov switching streams with sharp rows and four recurring laws, built to favour eras. They are a necessary condition. Language is untested.
  • Count tables do not generalise to text. For language, era heads must become closed-form ridge readouts on features. That is stage 7, not done.
  • The context control is small. The best context reader was a 0.86M transformer with a 512-token window. In-context learning grows with scale, and a larger or longer-context model could close the gap. That comparison is phase 2.
  • The filter is sequential. It runs token by token. The fix, a parallel scan in log space, is stage 6.
  • Regret is against the best path through the pool, not against the oracle. Closing that gap needs era-equals-regime estimation, which is cited, not formalised.
  • Some constants are tuned, not derived. δ = 0.05, a 32-token window and the 64-transition wait sit at the edges of their frozen grids. The 50/50 split between Q̃ and uniform in MOM was never tuned.
  • Lean proves the maths, not the Python. Property tests must pin the code's constants to each lemma's hypotheses, for example α ≤ 0.0078 for MOM's G2 guarantee.
  • The code still merges at BF > 1. L39d's guaranteed threshold is proved but not yet in arms.py.
  • The oracle and every count model share the generating prior (a = 0.2).
