## Amendment 11c (pre-result): trajectory-level calibration (L36 route R1)
Filed 2026-10-13, before any FCPA evaluation result on seeds 0, 1, 2, 4, 5 exists under the lag-stack input. The seed-0 pilot under the single-state input is disclosed in `PILOT_DISCLOSURE.md`; no eval-seed bed was read for this amendment. Appended to Amendments 11 (`FREEZE.md`), 11a (`FREEZE2.md`) and 11b (`FREEZE3.md`); none is edited. Where this text differs from them on how FCPA's conformal sets and its skill gate are calibrated, this text wins.

**What changes: the calibration data of FCPA.**
1. **No interleaved hold-out.** Every full window of every Train trajectory is a training window. The draft-era rule "window j of the stream is held out iff j mod 5 = 4" is withdrawn for the primary run (`FCPARegressor(holdout="trajectory")`).
2. **The Cal split calibrates.** After the 128 Train trajectories are fitted, the 64 Cal trajectories are passed once, in order, to `FCPARegressor.calibrate`. Each full window of w = 20 steps is scored by the law test; a window that a live era explains joins the calibration pool with that era's label and drives the online ACI step (L7). A window that no era explains, and the part-filled tail of each Cal trajectory, are discarded. Cal windows never enter era statistics, the time-traveller sums, the change test or a birth.
3. **Uncapped pool.** Every admitted Cal window is kept (about 64 × 19 × 20 = 24,320 rows at the pins). The 400-row FIFO of the interleaved rule is withdrawn for the primary run.
4. **Cluster-aware newborn rule.** Rows of one trajectory are not independent units (L36 H36-2). An era is calibrated only if its pool rows come from at least ⌈1/α⌉ − 1 = 9 distinct Cal trajectories; otherwise it is a newborn under L6′ and is in every set.
5. **The skill gate reads the same pool.** `_shrink` (the least-squares shrink toward persistence with `_MIN_GAIN = 0.2`) fits on the calibration pool of item 2, so its hold-out loss is an unbiased estimate of the fresh-trajectory loss (L36a).

**Cal has two roles, and neither reads Test.** The draft uses Cal for the era map only (majority regime at Cal steps, frozen before Test is read). Under 11c the same Cal trajectories also calibrate the conformal sets and the gate. Both roles read Cal inputs and, for the era map, Cal regime labels; the conformal labels are the model's own era labels, never regime labels. Test is read only after the era map, the conformal quantiles, the gate and the ACI state are fixed. So the reuse cannot leak Test. It couples the era map and the calibration through shared data, which changes no bar: P3 is scored on the router's argmin, which calibration does not touch (L36 H36-5), and P4 is the ACI guarantee, which holds for any base set.

**What does not change.** P1 to P4, C1, C2′, the beds, the eval seeds (0, 1, 2, 4, 5), the tuning seed (3), the 128/64/64 split, T = 25,600, the lag-stack input of 11b, the bootstrap, the Holm family S1 to S5, kill rules K1 to K9, and every pinned constant, including α = 0.1, γ = 0.05, w = 20, K_max = 8, Mondrian calibration, `_TAU` = 1.7, `_MIN_GAIN` = 0.2 and the damping pin 1.0. The κ, CTC-normalisation and law-threshold pins of the README plan are not part of this amendment.

**Why.** L36 proves that interleaving held-out windows with training windows of the same trajectory breaks the exchangeability that split conformal and the hold-out gate need (36b), and that whole-trajectory hold-out restores it with no mixing assumption (36c). Of the two whole-trajectory routes, R1 keeps every Train row; R2 (every 5th Train trajectory held out) costs 20 percent of them.

**Exploratory comparison behind the choice (not evidence for any bar).** `fcpa/explore_calibration.py` compared eight protocols on the tuning bed (seed 3, bed seed 2003) and on non-eval seed 7001 at full size; eval seeds were refused by the script. Static coverage of the true regime at α = 0.1 on the Test split, seed 3 / seed 7001: interleaved with the 400-row FIFO 0.856 / 0.765 (worst Test trajectory 0.07); interleaved uncapped 0.887 / 0.896; gap-1 0.919 / 0.939 (11 percent fewer training rows); R2 0.827 / 0.784; R1 0.800 / 0.853. No protocol brought E50 below persistence. With 64 Cal trajectories the L36c(ii) sampling slack is 0.153, so these two seeds do not separate R1 from the interleaved pool; R1 is chosen for its guarantee, not for these numbers. The calibration rule changes no pinned constant and was not chosen on any bar's value.

## Limits
- The coverage guarantee of 36c(ii) for an all-windows pool is at the trajectory level: with 64 Cal trajectories it holds at level 1 − α − 0.153 with probability 0.95. The pinned α is not adjusted for this.
- Cluster-aware n_min (item 4) was not part of the exploratory comparison.
- `run_primary.fcpa_train_macs` counts the Cal pass as training pairs (C2′ reports training MACs; no bar reads them).
