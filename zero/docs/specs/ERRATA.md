# FCPA errata and implementation deviations (post-freeze; the spec stays frozen per FREEZE.md)

Recorded 2026-10-11 by House from the builder reports. Each entry says what the code does, how it differs from the frozen spec, and why.

1. **L16 sign.** With later crossings composed on the left, the loop holonomy is exp(−½[A,B] + O(ε³)). The spec's +½[A,B] is the reversed composition order. Test: `test_L16_holonomy_measures_generator_noncommutativity_bch2`.
2. **TT in phase 1 is an LDA-posterior softmax over output bins, not an LLM.** `LogisticRegression` cannot make `partial_fit` exact (L27′), while LDA's additive statistics can. The LLM TT is the language-bed phase.
3. **TT-0 streaming state (m_t = Φ(m_{t−1}, x_t)) is not in the sklearn estimator**, because `check_estimator` requires row-wise `predict` invariance. Streaming lives outside the estimator, in the runner.
4. **Router score is a Mahalanobis distance to each era's Gaussian, not fidelity ψ†ρψ**, which could not separate eras whose states share one small subspace.
5. **Era detection in the estimator uses a Hoeffding test on the fraction of rows the open era explains**, through `bounds.certified_change`. Window-length Markov intervals over a 2-symbol alphabet never certify. `bounds.segment_markov` still exists for the Markov bed, and it needs w ≈ 100.
6. **Crossing operator: commuting diagonal generators at scale 0.3.** It is unitary and wired in, but near identity, so it changes little here. Learning G_a (L18\*: max tr(V_k V_k† S ρ_j S†)) is not implemented yet.
7. **Not implemented:** the Naimark ancilla extension for exhausted rank(E_∅); the N/τ_min birth cap (replaced by a plain `n_max` cap); L12 (window ≥ mixing time); wiring the holonomy to the `det ∏G` readout.
8. **L24\* power.** On null data the product martingale drifts down about 0.19 per step at κ = 0.5, so after long quiet stretches a real change takes hundreds of steps to trigger a birth. **Decision (House): keep the Ville guarantee** (no floor or restart). Detection delay is reported in the S2 analysis. The total false-birth probability is ≤ η + δ, where η = δ/2 is the DKW calibration slack.
9. **`parseval_frame` raises when K + 1 < N**, because V†V = I is impossible there.
10. **L-GIVEN REFUSE band.** As written, PRESENT/SMALL leaves no REFUSE region, so REFUSE fires when A is rank-deficient or the caller's `scale` is below 2η.
11. **`segment` cannot see boundaries within w steps of either end**, and exactness needs eras of at least w steps.
12. **`FCPARegressor` gained the parameters `y_edges` and `n_bins`.** Exactness against `fit` needs shared output bins; use `FCPARegressor.make_edges(y, n_bins)`.
13. **Dr House H1 (perturbation-theory crossing) died at its stated bar.** At ε = 0.05 it recovers 0.781 of the oracle gain against a 0.90 bar, at 2000 ψ. It passes at ε = 0.1 (0.944) and ε = 0.2 (0.984), and the sinΘ Finset bound holds in 60/60 seeds. Cause: a sampling floor. With the exact ΔE it recovers 0.9999/0.9997/0.9984. Repriced, it stands as "exact in the noise-free limit, sample-limited otherwise", with recovery measured at 0.885 (4000 ψ) and 0.944 (8000 ψ); those were measured after the fact and are not a pass. The ε = 0.05 case is kept as a strict xfail. The "beats PCA_j" claim stays dead.
14. **Dr House H3 (mixture-over-change-time e-detector) is BOUND.** Median delay after a 1e4 null stretch is 23 steps against 2392 for the product martingale. The false-birth rate is 41/2000 = 0.0205 against a 0.0597 bar, and M_0 = 1 with the martingale property checked by Monte Carlo (κ ≥ 0.8; κ = 0.5 has infinite variance). This supersedes ERRATA 8: the Ville guarantee is kept AND detection is fast.
15. **Dr House H4 (Hilbert-metric CTC) is DEAD at T = 1 and BOUND at T = 1.25, repriced into a design rule.** At T = 1 the map is genuinely d_H-expansive: κ/T = 1.1255, measured max ratio 1.12548, and 3002 of 1e4 pairs have ratio > 1. At T = 1.25, κ/T = 0.9004 with measured max 0.90038 (tight), and the undamped (ε = 0) loop converges to a 1e-10 d_H certificate in 19–20 iterations from every start. **Rule: choose the post-selection temperature T > κ_H = (β/4)·max_{ijkl}(A_ik − A_jk − A_il + A_jl) (nurse e8: exact constant). The CTC loop is then a certified contraction of the operator itself, with no damping and no 2ε/(1−c) bias.** Top-m is a fixed soft positive mask (a d_H isometry); hard top-m flips are outside the claim. New Lean target L22H: in the oscillation norm on log-coordinates, softmax(βA·)/T is (κ_H/T)-Lipschitz.
16. **Dr House H2 (attention TT, with the KV multiset as the sufficient statistic) is BOUND on exactness.** fit equals shuffled 10-chunk partial_fit bitwise; the explicit reference matches within 1e-12; check_estimator gives 54 passed and 1 skipped (corrected per AUDIT_2; originally logged as 55). On NLL it passes 12 of 13 streams (seeds 10–19: mean margin 0.314 nats/token, minimum 0.167, against the LDA TT and the bigram). Seed 2 misses (margin 0.084 < 0.10) and is kept as a strict xfail. Hyperparameters were tuned on validation seed 100. **Limit:** the margin depends on how sharply the chain is order-2. With an EMA history key, flatter chains (concentration 0.4) fail. The replacement route is a lag-stack key x_t = [e_t, e_{t−1}] (exploratory probe, optimistic because β was picked on test). `AttentionTT` is in `fcpa/tt_attention.py`, as a TT candidate beside the LDA TT.
17. **Nurse e4 (closed-form zoom):** S = U_k U_0^H from cached eigenbases reaches the Ky Fan ceiling exactly at about 68× less cost than expm (N = 16), but it maps every era's chart onto the same span, so zoom alone cannot discriminate eras. This corroborates ERRATA 13: "expansion" is alignment, and era discrimination must come from the law fingerprint (L14′), not from the zoom.
