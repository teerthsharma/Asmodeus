## Definitions in Seal's own words

- **TT.** "Our softmax is a TT". It "is smart alone"; when it needs knowledge of a particular era, "he goes back in time". The TT holds "only results of history".
- **Era.** An era "contains knowledge", like a JEPA. "JEPAs are eras inside". Going to an era "expands" what the TT knows.
- **Fluid / liquid.** The TT is given "a dimensional phase", and its "momentum will be like in a dimension of liquid". Combined with the "path of softmax", this gives a "liquid of JEPA".
- **Architecture.** "Fluid chrononal predictive architecture (after multiplication and normalisation)."
- **Decider.** "Small phases all around the sphere, but the decider will be a softmax". The observer is a "T-softmax observer".
- **Constraints.**
  - Not JEPA, nor self attention.
  - The internal design is "butterfly effect bonded via topology".
  - Topology is the aim.
- **Method.** Research → LEMMA and LEAN → BUILD → PROVEN.

The exact phrase "chronal fluid data density" does not appear in either note. The closest words are "liquid of JEPA", "momentum … in a dimension of liquid" and "fluid chrononal".
