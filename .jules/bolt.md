## 2026-03-30 - Analytical Shortcuts for Unitary Operator Projections and Ungrouped Reductions
**Learning:** `diag_q_instrument` in quantum instrument checks originally computed 4,096 full SVDs per call to find operator norm bounds (`||K_v||_2^2`). Because `Q` is unitary, `||diag(s) Q||_2` is analytically equal to `max_i |s_i|`, reducing O(V * N^3) SVD computation to O(V) array max. Similarly, in SIGReg regularisation, computing ungrouped regulariser losses via atomic `index_add` scatters on zero tensors is significantly slower than direct `.mean(dim=0, keepdim=True)` reductions.
**Action:** Always check if structured operator classes (like unitary-transformed diagonals or single-group scatter indices) admit analytical/direct reduction fast-paths before delegating to general numerical algorithms like SVD or atomic scatters.
